# ApexCraft Studios - Digital Agency & Portfolio

A modern, high-performance web agency portfolio showcasing digital craftsmanship, custom enterprise design, and 6 bespoke interactive client applications.

## 🌟 Featured Interactive Projects

Each project features a dedicated, fully interactive demo:

1. **ApexCare Dental Clinics** (`demos/apexcare.html`) - Patient-centric dental care platform with online appointment booking, doctor profiles, and service calculator.
2. **ApexLuxe Apparel** (`demos/apexluxe.html`) - Luxury fashion e-commerce storefront with interactive cart, seasonal curation, and quick view modals.
3. **ApexFinova Analytics** (`demos/apexfinova.html`) - High-velocity financial analytics and enterprise reporting dashboard with real-time charts and KPI metrics.
4. **ApexVanguard Law Partners** (`demos/apexvanguard.html`) - Premier corporate legal consultancy portal with case evaluation intake and legal practice directories.
5. **ApexPrime Luxury Realty** (`demos/apexprime.html`) - High-end architectural real estate showcase with property filtering, photo galleries, and private tour booking.
6. **ApexSonic Precision Audio** (`demos/apexsonic.html`) - Audiophile hardware store with 360° product showcases, interactive frequency equalizer, and spec comparisons.
